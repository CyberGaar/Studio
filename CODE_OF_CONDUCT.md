# Community Code of Conduct

CyberGaar is committed to a respectful, safe, and technically rigorous community.

## Expected conduct

- Treat participants with respect regardless of background, identity, experience, or viewpoint.
- Give actionable technical feedback and separate critique of work from criticism of people.
- Protect confidential reports, credentials, customer information, and personal data.
- Respect project scope, maintainer decisions, licenses, and coordinated-disclosure timelines.
- Disclose conflicts of interest and correct mistakes openly.

## Unacceptable conduct

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, deliberate disruption, retaliation, unauthorized security testing, and publication of private vulnerability or personal information are prohibited.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer listed in `MAINTAINERS.md`. Maintainers may warn, restrict, temporarily suspend, or permanently remove participation based on severity and pattern. Anyone with a conflict of interest must recuse themselves from the response.

Security vulnerabilities must follow `SECURITY.md`, not the conduct-reporting process.
